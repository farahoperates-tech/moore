# MOORE — THE BIBLE (v1)
*a pixel odyssey · Mode 13h · one soul, seven spirits, a dying sun*

## LOGLINE
Long after the sea gave the drowned world back, the first and only enduring forest —
a tired goddess who has carried the whole memory of humankind as a curse — wills a
single human into being, fully grown, doomed *to live*, to gather her seven ancient
spirits from the seven ruins of the earth and scatter them back into the ground. Only
then can she lay down her burden, follow the vanished moon into oblivion, and let a
younger moon — and a newer world — be born.

## COSMOLOGY
- The **moon was never heaven** — a place, a portal, and the true **source of the sun's light**.
- **Visitors came in peace** and took a **piece** of the moon. The moon **left** into oblivion; the
  sun **died of grief**, but slowly, core-outward — its last light still crawling toward us. **The
  world is going dark in real time.** That is the clock under everything.
- Before leaving, the moon made the **forest** promise to keep the world's spirits safe. Humanity
  drowned, spent uncounted years on floating vessels; the forest endured, holding all seven spirits.
  Spirits cannot be destroyed — *you cannot destroy what you do not understand.*
- The goddess is exhausted. She scatters her seven through a messenger, keeps her promise, is freed —
  and if the messenger succeeds, a **new younger moon** is granted, with new spirits, and the world
  begins anew: new light, plants, warmth, love, music.

## PROTAGONIST — *you*
Born a full-grown adult, genderless, wearing **Farah's** features (long dark hair, long neck, big
dark eyes). Not an amnesiac — you **never had** a memory. Doomed to live and to carry. You have done
this before; the failed ones are the **followers** in the wood, and they are all *you*.

## OBJECT
Gather the **seven spirits**. Each makes you **heavier** — slower, sadder, more human — because
**knowledge is weight and grief is power; the same stat.** Carry all seven, scatter them, free the
goddess, earn the new moon.

## THE SEVEN
Four gifts + a shadow family of three (Evil, mother of Fire and the Storm):
- **Knowledge** — to know; boots the world up. Prophetic, tells the truth ("go south").
- **Light** — art, music, growth, memory of beauty. Warm, mournful.
- **Life** — being, dying, the circle. Gentle, of growth and rot.
- **Love** — friendship, the thing worth living for. Tender, fleeting, childlike.
- **Storm (Wind & Water, one)** — Evil's daughter, Fire's sister; grief that carries you. Raging, then soothing.
- **Evil** — the mother; cannot be killed, only understood. Loud, cruel, deceiving — softens at the very end.
- **Fire** — Evil's daughter; the last warmth in a dying-sun world. **Silent, only ever burning.**

## THE VOICES (written text — audio parked)
Before **Knowledge**: no words, only the **wind moving on screen** and the **scent** of a nearby soul.
After Knowledge: the world speaks as **text under the interface** — *"the wind speaks: …"*,
*"Knowledge whispers from a dream: go south…"*, *"Evil laughs: go east, where you die."* Unattributed
at first; with time you learn each tongue. Evil is loudest and lies; Knowledge tells the truth; Fire
never speaks. (One day voiced in Farah's real cloned voice — a later pass, no rented hardware needed.)

## THE RECORD OF THE WORLD THAT ONCE WAS (map & movement)
- **Within a continent:** top-down, room-to-room, survive the season, fight what guards the soul, follow the scent.
- **Between continents:** you **sail**, and the **wind decides**. Until the Storm is tamed she wrecks you
  (this is how you crash into the Amazon). Tamed, she carries you (Drake Passage → Antarctica; the dead
  Pacific → Fire). **The Storm-spirit's arc IS the travel system.**
- The **Record** is a weathered chart in the HUD, filling in continent by continent.

## THE JOURNEY
1. **RUSSIA — autumn→winter → KNOWLEDGE.** The dark room; learn to see, kill, survive cold. High in the
   **mountains**, take Knowledge — and the world *boots up* (voices, XP, Record, dreams).
2. **EUROPE — deep winter → LIGHT.** Down the mountains to Greece/the Mediterranean: art, music, growth,
   tragedies of a forgotten past and a wanted future.
3. **THE WRECK → AMAZON → LIFE.** Sailing for North America, the wind (not yet yours) wrecks you into South
   America. A small friend attaches to you. To know life you must know death: the friend **dies**, your own
   spirit is **born**, and you take Life.
4. **AFRICA — high summer → LOVE.** Only anguish, no hope — which is why love must come. Desert, gold
   mountains, then the savannah's bliss. Love needs someone to live for: **the woman of duality**, guide and
   ruin, even the reason your friend died. When you don't want her to **die**, you **stop fighting** — and take Love.
5. **AUSTRALIA → THE STORM.** It's all too much. Memories at once, wind, rain; you fall to your knees — *I
   can't anymore* — and the Storm **consumes** you. Surrender *is* the taking. Then, heard at last, she softens.
6. **RETURN TO THE AMAZON.** Now that you know love, you **bury your friend.** Now it can rest.
7. **ANTARCTICA — via the Drake Passage, the storm with you → EVIL.** You've carried her cruel voice since
   the first dark room. You don't slay her — you **understand and carry** her. The voice goes quiet, then kind, then free.
8. **ACROSS THE DEAD PACIFIC — past the wreck of the station *Elpis* → FIRE.** All seven awake, the lost
   history floods in. Take the last silent burning spirit, **scatter all seven** — and then **you die too,
   and your spirit is the seed of all the new spirits.** The goddess is freed; a new moon rises; the world begins again.

## TWO LAWS (locked)
- **Time moves with distance.** The deeper you travel, the more the **season turns** — every stretch of
  rooms rusts autumn toward winter toward blizzard. Place *is* time; walking *is* the calendar.
- **The final death is yours.** When the seven are scattered, **the player must die too** — and *your*
  spirit becomes the **seed of all the new spirits**, the first soul poured into the new moon. No life
  without death. The messenger is the last sacrifice and the first seed.

## THE FOUND HISTORY (fragments, strongest on the Pacific crossing)
- ~2,300 The Warming — the coasts drown.
- ~4,000 The Silence — the old networks die; knowledge shatters into rumor.
- ~6,500 The Age of Godheads — minds built and worshipped, then buried.
- ~12,000 The Ashfall — a mountain unmakes a decade of summers.
- ~19,000 The Salt Kingdoms — empires rise and fall with the tides, forgetting each other.
- 26,000 The Great Plague — the last enormous dying.
- ~28,500 The Gene-Courts — hereditary disease ended forever.
- ~31,000 The *Elpis* — a station among the stars; later it falls into the Pacific.
- 32,700 The Unity — one people, no war, no sickness. *We were so close.*
- 33,000 The Visitors, the Shattering, the Leaving.
- The Uncounted Years — no calendar survives. The sea rose, then fell. The forest waited, then wished for you.

## SYSTEMS
- **Survival — full.** Warmth, hunger, thirst can kill. Each season is a different way to die.
- **Combat — real.** Cursor teaches the knife; weapons **wear and break**. **No XP until Knowledge** —
  before it you learn only by doing.
- **Hearts.** Each spirit taken = **+1 life**, because the world **notices** you more (watchful rangers,
  strong ones, bosses).
- **Scent.** You *smell* souls; proximity intensifies no matter the distance.
- **The Knowledge Gate.** The HUD is systems-light until Knowledge, then it visibly **boots up** — the
  terminal aesthetic made diegetic.

## THE WOMAN OF DUALITY
One figure, every realm — guide and undoing, the same white worn two ways, because her duality *is* the
duality of everything. In Africa she is the friend, the one worth living for, the one you must stop fighting.

## TONE & LOOK
Mode 13h 320×200; terminal frame; the storybook charm of **Rhapsody** married to the chorus-in-your-head
of **Hellblade** (as text). The world grows **literally darker** each continent as the sun dies, until
**Fire** is the last warmth. **Music comes last.**

## BUILD PLAN
Vertical slice first: **Continent One — Russia / Knowledge** (systems-light by design), then scale the

## CHAPTER ONE — THE QUEST CANON (locked 2026-08)
Chapter One (Asia) is deliberately **simple**: it is the chapter of *learning*. Later continents grow complex; here the player learns to live and to KNOW. The spirit of Knowledge = **learning to READ**. Before it, written script shows as unreadable glyphs and dwellers only point; each language-fragment resolves more of it; taking Knowledge boots the whole UI online.

### The 7 Memory-Fragments (gather all → the guardian's exam is passed → take Knowledge)
1. **The First Mark** — the Crystal Cavern / deep cave: the painted hand, symbol before language.
2. **The First Voice** — the Mongolian steppe: the sung epic, memory before writing.
3. **The First Page** — drowned Beijing / China: oracle bone and the printed word.
4. **The Nothing (zero)** — India: the number, and abstraction; India also teaches MEDICINE.
5. **The Alphabet** — Samarkand, the crossroads: the shapes that hold sound. *Unlocks READING.*
6. **The Book** — Mesopotamia (Uruk/Babylon): found, unreadable until you have the Alphabet; once read, it says GO NORTH.
7. **The First Grief** — the Levant (Palestine): read the sorrow in the ruins; needs reading; the evil winds begin here.

### Systems (Chapter One)
- **Four survival stats:** warmth, hunger, thirst, and **stamina** (drains very slowly; restored by SLEEPING in a house).
- **Houses:** enter → interior screen → sleep. Sleep restores stamina + warmth; heals wounds only after MEDICINE is learned in India.
- **Wayshrines:** scattered checkpoints; touch to bind your soul → death returns you there, not to the start.
- **Backpack (10 slots):** inherited from the dead traveller (with the map); holds clothes, spare weapons, food, medicine.
- **Climate clothes:** the coat warms the north but overheats you in the south; find **desert wraps** in hot-land chests. Clothes come from **chests** and **dead travellers**.
- **The Record:** a weathered chart of drowned Asia — coastlines eaten by the sea, dead airports, olden streets — your route inked in, a ✶ glyph pulsing on every region that still holds an uncollected fragment.
- **The guardian** is the exam: it stands aside only when all 7 fragments are gathered.
